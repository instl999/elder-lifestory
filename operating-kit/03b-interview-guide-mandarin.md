# Interview guide — Mandarin

The Mandarin companion to [`03-interview-guide-standard.md`](03-interview-guide-standard.md),
for an operator who reads English and interviews in Mandarin. The same guide, entirely in
Chinese, is [`zh/03-访谈指南.md`](zh/03-访谈指南.md).

Read the standard guide first: the craft is the same — short questions, one follow-up at a
time, looking for **scenes** rather than summary, stopping when they tire. This guide covers
what is different when the story owner is an older Chinese speaker, and gives the questions
in Chinese.

The questions are **adapted, not translated.** "What did you do on a Sunday?" means little to
someone who grew up in 1940s China; "小时候过年是什么样子的？" opens almost everyone.

---

## Before the first session

**Ask the family how they like to be addressed (称呼), and use it every time.** Never the full
name alone. 王奶奶, 王老师, 老王, 王阿姨, 陈伯伯 — whichever the family uses, or whatever they
were called at work if that was a source of pride (a retired teacher is often still 老师).
Always 您, never 你. Record it with `lifestory new --preferred "王奶奶"`; the book's
confirmation sheet and front matter use it.

**Ask about dialect.** Many people of this generation speak Mandarin as a second language.
If they are more at ease in 上海话, 粤语, 闽南话, 四川话 or another dialect:

- Whisper will struggle — expect heavy correction, and set `--language` honestly on the case.
- Consider having a family member who speaks the dialect in the room *to translate only*.
- A story told fluently in dialect is worth more than a halting one in Mandarin. Let them
  choose.

**Offer tea, and do not rush.** A session that opens with ten minutes of 家常 (small talk) is
not wasted. Starting straight on the questions reads as an interrogation.

---

## What is different

**Modesty is the first answer, not the last.** "我这辈子没什么好说的", "都是普通人的事" —
this is courtesy, not a closed door. Do not argue. Ask about something concrete instead:
"那您小时候家里吃什么？" The stories arrive once the question is small enough.

**Speak with them alone when you can.** Many older people will not speak freely in front of
their children, and especially not in front of a son- or daughter-in-law. A family member in
the room changes what gets said.

**Let them lead into the hard years.** For this generation, a life story passes through the
famine years, the political campaigns, 下乡, separation from family. Do not ask about these
directly. If the story owner goes there, listen, and let them set the depth. Never press.

Anything about *who did what to whom* — denunciations, persecution, a relative's role on
either side — is §12.2 material **by default**: mark the card restricted even if they do not
ask you to, and ask them plainly, later, whether it belongs in a family book. Living families
are affected by these accounts, and the story owner may not have thought about that in the
moment of telling it.

**Numbers and dates come in many forms.** 五七年, 一九五七年, 那年我十九岁, 属狗的那年,
农历八月十五. All are real dates to the speaker. Record what they said; resolve the calendar
year later, in the confirmation sheet, not in the interview.

## Opening a session (开场)

Safe, sensory, concrete. Never open on hardship.

- 小时候过年是什么样子的？年夜饭吃什么？
- 家里谁做饭最好吃？拿手菜是什么？
- 小时候家里的灶台是什么样的？现在闻到什么味道，会让您想起那时候？
- 您第一次拿到自己挣的钱，买了什么？

If they brought photographs, start there. A photograph in the hand is the most reliable way
into a memory.

## Questions by territory

### Childhood and home 童年与老家

- 您小时候住的地方，现在闭上眼睛还能看见的，是什么？
- 那时候家里住了几口人？晚上都怎么睡？
- 家里有什么规矩，是您小时候绝对不敢违反的？
- 小时候有没有哪里，是大人不让您去的？
- 下雨天、冬天，你们小孩都玩些什么？

### Family 家人

- 您觉得自己最像家里的谁？
- 您母亲生气的时候，会怎么样？
- 家里有没有为了什么事闹过意见？
- 家里有没有哪个人，是大家不太提起的？
  *(Near private territory. Ask once, gently, take the first answer. §12.2 by default.)*

### Work 工作

- 您第一份活是什么？拿工分还是拿工资？一个月挣多少？
- 是谁教您干这个的？
- 干那份活的时候，有没有哪天出了岔子？
- 单位里有没有您特别怕的人？
- 您什么时候觉得，自己把这份活干好了？

### Turning points — where the book lives 人生转折

- 您这辈子做过的最大胆的一件事是什么？
- **有没有哪一次，您没有按家里给您安排的路走？**
  *(The strongest question in the bank. It produces a whole scene almost unprompted.)*
- 有没有什么决定，到现在您还会想起来？
- 有没有哪一刻，您知道日子要变了？

### Marriage 婚姻

- 您和老伴是怎么认识的？是别人介绍的，还是自己认识的？
- 第一次见面是在哪儿？您当时觉得他（她）怎么样？
- 家里人当时觉得他（她）怎么样？
- 他（她）私底下是个什么样的人？
- 你们为什么事拌过嘴？

*(Ask the second question even if the answer to the first was 介绍的. An arranged first
meeting is often the most vivid scene in the whole book.)*

### That era — let them lead 那个年代

Offer these as doors, not as a checklist. Take whichever one they walk through.

- 您还记得用粮票、布票的时候吗？一个月能领多少？
- 家里第一件"大件"是什么？自行车、缝纫机、手表，还是收音机？是怎么买到的？
- 你们那时候结婚，嫁妆和彩礼是什么？
- 您那一辈有没有人下过乡？在哪儿？
- 您第一次坐火车，是去哪儿？

### Objects and places 物件与地方

- 如果家里着火，有什么东西您一定会冲回去拿？
- 您小时候住的那条街，那时候是什么样子？
- 有没有什么东西，现在没有了，您很怀念？

### Late sessions, once trust exists 建立信任之后

- 如果回到二十岁，您最想告诉自己什么？
- 您最希望孙辈们了解您的什么？
- 有没有什么事，您从来没跟别人说过？
  *(Ask once, near the end of a later session, then be quiet. Whatever comes back is §12.2
  material and stays with them unless they release it. Do not follow up; do not ask again.)*

### Closing every session 结束

- 今天还有什么是我该问、却没问到的？
- 下次我们聊点什么？

## Assisted mode (辅助模式, §12.3)

The rules in [`04-interview-guide-assisted.md`](04-interview-guide-assisted.md) apply
unchanged. Closed question first, then open. Never correct. Ten to fifteen minutes.

- 这是您母亲吗？ → 她是个什么样的人？
- 这张照片是在哪儿拍的？ → 那天发生了什么？
- 您小时候有自行车吗？ → 骑着去哪儿？
- 那时候冬天怎么取暖？
- 您能比划一下，那个活是怎么干的吗？
- 有没有一首歌，那时候大家都会唱？
  *(Music outlasts almost everything else. Sometimes they will sing it; record it.)*

Dialect matters even more here: as recall weakens, people fall back on the language of their
childhood. If they drift from Mandarin into dialect, let them.

## Logging questions

Log every question in the language you asked it, so the question bank learns which Chinese
questions produce scenes:

```bash
python -m lifestory ask <case-id> "有没有哪一次，您没有按家里给您安排的路走？" --bank-id q-turn-went-against
```

The seed bank (`question-bank/seed.yaml`) carries the Chinese version of each question in its
`zh` field.
