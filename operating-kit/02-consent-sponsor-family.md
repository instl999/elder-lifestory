## Part A — for the sponsor

### What you are buying

A memoir of ______________________'s life, produced from recorded
conversations with them, and delivered as a printed book.

Package: ______  Price: ______  Expected delivery: ______________

### What you are not buying

This is the part people are surprised by, so we say it plainly and up front.

**You will not receive the recordings or transcripts.** They belong to the
person being interviewed. You will receive the finished book and the stories
they have chosen to share.

**You do not decide what goes in the book. They do.** If they tell us something
they would rather you did not know, we keep it, and we will not tell you that
there was anything to keep.

**We may decline to include material you want included**, if they have not
agreed to it.

If this is not what you expected, say so now rather than later. It is not
negotiable, but it is much better discussed today.

- [ ] I understand and accept this.

### What we need from you

- Photographs, documents and dates where you have them
- Correct spellings of names — we will send a short list of about 25 things to
  confirm, which takes roughly 15 minutes. **This is the only substantial task
  we will ask of you.**
- Approval of the book's direction before chapters are written
- An hour or so of your time, in total

### If something happens

If ______________________ dies, becomes unable to continue, or withdraws
before the book is finished:

- You receive everything completed, finished to the best standard the material
  allows, at **no further charge**
- Anything unpaid is written off
- Anything they marked private stays private, permanently

Full policy: `06-policy-story-owner-dies.md`, available on request.

## Notes for the operator

- If the sponsor pushes back on not receiving transcripts, hold the line. This
  is §12.2 and it is the product. A sponsor who cannot accept it is a customer
  we decline.
- Watch for the family member who wants a particular grievance in the book.
  Their recollection is recorded and attributed; it does not become the story
  owner's account.
- If contributors disagree about a photograph's subjects, that becomes a
  confirmation-queue item — do not resolve it yourself.
