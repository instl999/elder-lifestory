# Printer specification

- Trim size: 6.0″ × 9.0″
- Estimated page count: 19 (round up to a multiple of 4)
- Estimated spine width: 0.162″ at 80gsm cream
- Interior margins: 0.9″ inner, 0.75″ outer/top/bottom
- Bleed: 0.125″ on all outer edges for any full-bleed photograph
- Body text: 12pt SimSun (Chinese) with Times New Roman (Latin) (set for older readers)
- Binding: case laminate hardcover
- Colour: interior mono unless photographs are placed in a colour section
- Export the DOCX to PDF from Word or WPS with fonts embedded before sending

Confirm the spine width with the printer once the final page count is fixed.

If printing in mainland China, check the printer's content-review requirements before promising a delivery date: some print shops review privately printed books, and a memoir that touches on sensitive historical periods can be delayed or refused.

## Too short to case-bind

At roughly 19 pages this is below the ~32-page minimum most print-on-demand services will hardcover.

Options, in the order worth trying:

1. **Go back for more material.** A thin book usually means thin interviews, not a thin life. The direction's `gaps` list says where.
2. **Add photographs with full captions** — they carry story and pages at once.
3. **Saddle-stitch or softcover instead**, and say so to the family before they see it rather than after.

For an assisted-mode case (§12.3) a short book may be the honest outcome. Set that expectation with the sponsor early.
