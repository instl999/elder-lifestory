# Consent — the story owner

中文版：[01-知情同意书-讲述人.md](zh/01-知情同意书-讲述人.md)

> **This is a working template, not a legal document.** It has not been
> reviewed by a lawyer. Before you use it with a paying customer, have it
> reviewed in your jurisdiction — particularly the data-protection, image-rights
> and capacity provisions. Spec §22 lists archive ownership and long-term
> preservation as open legal questions.

**How to use it:** read it aloud, in the room, in your own words. Do not hand
it over to be signed unread. If the person cannot follow it when read aloud,
that is itself information about capacity (§12.3).

---

## What we are doing

**Project:** ______________________  **Date:** ______________

We are going to record conversations with you about your life, and use them to
make a book for your family.

You will be asked questions. You can answer any of them, or none of them.

---

## What happens to what you say

**The recordings are yours.** They are not given to your family. Not to the
person paying for this. Not to anyone, unless you say so.

**Nothing goes in the book unless you agree to it.** We will go through what
you have told us, piece by piece, and you decide what belongs in the book and
what stays between us.

**If you tell us something you do not want your family to know, that is yours
and it stays yours.** It will not appear in the book, it will not be mentioned
to them, and we will not raise it with you again unless you do.

**One person other than you will read the recordings**: the editor putting the
book together. They are bound to keep it confidential and will not pass any of
it on.

---

## What we will use

Please tick what you are happy with. You can change any of these later.

- [ ] **Recording** our conversations (audio)
- [ ] **Computer assistance** — we use software to help organise what you tell
      us and to help draft the writing. A person reads and checks everything
      before you or your family see it.
- [ ] **Sending recordings to an outside company** — that software is run by
      a company outside this project. To use it, the words you say are sent to
      their computers. They are: ______________________ (operator: name the
      provider and the country their servers are in). They keep it under their
      own rules, not ours.
- [ ] **Sharing with family** — the finished book and the parts of your stories
      you have approved
- [ ] **Printing** the book
- [ ] Photographs you or your family provide
- [ ] Something wider than family (we will ask you again separately if this
      ever comes up)

We will **not** use your stories to train computer systems, and we will not
give them to anyone outside this project.

---

## Changing your mind

You can stop at any point, for any reason, without explaining.

You can ask us to remove anything, at any time, including after the book is
printed. We will remove it from our records and tell you what we have done.

You can ask for a copy of everything we hold about you, in a form you can keep.

**Who should have this archive after you?** This matters and we would rather
ask now than guess later.

## If you are not sure

It is completely fine to say:

- "I don't remember"
- "Skip that one"
- "Keep that private"
- "Not today"

None of these are a problem. We will not press.
